Quote Tape - every bid, ask and trade update, bucketed into 5s bars, simulated 100ms feed

Bid/ask: WebglSegments thin horizontal lines spanning the bar, colored lighter at the start of the bar and darker toward its end. Trades: one WebglDots dot per bar at the volume-weighted average price, diameter ∝ log(total size). Axes: 2D canvas overlay.

Bar interval
bid (start → end of bar) ask (start → end of bar) trades per bar (VWAP), total size → diameter
